# AV1 Delivery Checklist

## Gate purpose

AV1 is the first academic delivery. This checklist controls presence and
reviewability; it does not convert a prepared Markdown section into an
accepted submission.

| Gate | Evidence required | Current state | Closure proof |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Front matter | Cover, version history, collaboration insights, contents, Student Outcome and SMART objectives | Structure present; official identities and contribution evidence pending | Reviewed report export and team confirmation |
| Chapters I–II | Presentation, target segments, competitors, interviews, needfinding and requirements | Source reconciliation present; primary interview and owner review pending | Approved artifacts, provenance and manual review |
| Requirements | 28 Mobile V1 stories, 112 scenarios, Impact Map and Product Backlog | Structural validators pass; `28/28` report integration approved; individual defense follow-up | Story register with reviewer/date/decision |
| Conclusions | Problem statements, assumptions, hypotheses, success criteria and roadmap | Provisional only | Validation-backed review and accepted decisions |
| Bibliography | APA 7, four mandatory papers, category annexes and body citations | Metadata/DOI and citation gate pass; Q1/Q2 official capture pending | Official indexing capture and visual PDF review |
| Annexes | Interview/needfinding/DDD/UX/ethics evidence with source and permission | Register present; evidence pending | Artifact links, screenshots, consent and reviewer |
| Submission package | PDF, presentation/video links and classroom upload | Not produced in this cut | Accessible links, page-number and visual checks |
