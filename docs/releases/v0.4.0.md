# Nexa Mobile Report v0.4.0

## Scope

`v0.4.0` applies a surgical content-consistency correction to the public
report while preserving the Product Backlog, interview records, Jira evidence,
video evidence and four-Sprint allocation.

## Release identity

- Integrated report baseline on `develop`
- Release branch: `release/0.4.0`
- Immutable release tag: `v0.4.0`

## Included work

- Aligned the Impact Mapping narrative for Carlos Mendoza with
  `MOB-US-044`, `MOB-US-047`, `MOB-US-048` and `MOB-US-049`.
- Corrected B2B Buyers traceability, Sellable Availability, Payment Provider
  terminology and the onboarding actor sequence.

  onboarding render after clarifying authority boundaries.
- Removed stale Chapter III counts and historical-site wording.
- Kept 97 Product Backlog elements, 426 story points and exactly four academic
  Sprints unchanged.

## Verification

- Checked Markdown whitespace, relative assets, Mermaid sources, SVG/XML and
  signed Git history before publication.
- Confirmed no interview, Jira, video, Product Backlog or Sprint evidence was
  modified by this release.

## Evidence boundaries

- UXPressia exports remain research and design artifacts; they do not prove
  prototype execution, validation or implementation.
- The existing Impact Mapping PNG remains unchanged because its editable source
  was not available; the aligned textual narrative does not claim that visual
  asset was regenerated.
- This release does not claim a Mobile implementation, device build,
  deployment, user validation or new interview findings.
