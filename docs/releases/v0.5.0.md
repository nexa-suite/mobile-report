# Nexa Mobile Report v0.5.0

## Scope

`v0.5.0` closes the Phase A and Phase B academic baseline for the Nexa Mobile
Report. It integrates the current report branches, preserves accepted research,
requirements, Product Backlog and DDD decisions, and publishes the AV1
composition without presenting planning or research as solution validation.

## Release identity

- Integrated report baseline: `develop` after the current Phase A + B branch integration
- Release branch: `release/0.5.0`
- Immutable release tag: `v0.5.0`

## Included work

- Completed and reconciled 9/9 formative Needfinding interviews across the three
  target segments (3/3/3), including Personas, User Task Matrix, Journeys,
  As-Is Scenario Mapping and Empathy Mapping.
- Reconciled Buyer evidence around replenishment, supply continuity, punctuality,
  supplier coordination and availability for the Buyer's own customers; receipt
  and discrepancy remain valid evidence without becoming the whole segment.
- Reconciled research disposition, As-Is → To-Be framing, requirements and the
  textual interpretation of Impact Mapping without redrawing accepted DDD
  boundaries or converting H1, H2 or H3 into solution validation.
- Preserved the Product Backlog at 97 PBIs / 426 SP and Sprint 1 at 6 PBIs / 19
  SP, with Jira-backed Product Backlog and Sprint traceability evidence.
- Preserved explicit evidence boundaries for development, testing, execution,
  services, deployment and collaboration; these references do not claim a
  completed Mobile client or production runtime.
- Kept the default AV1 composition scoped to front matter, Chapters I–II,
  conclusions, glossary, bibliography and annexes; Chapter III and Chapter IV
  source directories remain available but are excluded from AV1 navigation and
  export.

## Verification

- Verified the nine interview records and 3/3/3 segment coverage, the research
  analysis, backlog totals and Sprint 1 allocation.
- Verified relative links and assets, Markdown whitespace, export-script syntax,
  AV1 Chapter III/IV exclusion, clean worktrees and signed release commits.
- Verified the annotated `v0.5.0` tag and its published GitHub Release after
  publication.

## Evidence boundaries

- Needfinding evidence informs users, problems and tasks; it remains distinct
  from solution validation. H1, H2 and H3 are not marked solution-validated.
- UXPressia visual refinements, final screenshot/title-card work and any device,
  deployment or production validation remain separate manual evidence actions.
- The consolidated interview video is accepted as a long recording and is not
  treated as a release defect.
- No fabricated quotes, public-anonymous Jira access, device results or
  production claims are introduced by this release.
