# Nexa Mobile Report v0.6.0

AV1 Academic Closeout

## Scope

`v0.6.0` is the integrated AV1 academic closeout baseline for Nexa Mobile. It
consolidates the completed report evidence without introducing new product scope.

## Included

- Completed formative research: 9/9 interviews, with three records for each
  target segment.
- Reconciled Needfinding, with As-Is and To-Be traceability aligned.
- Preserved User Stories, Technical Stories, and Spikes.
- Product Backlog preserved at 97 PBIs / 426 SP.
- Strategic and tactical DDD preserved with consolidated architecture evidence.
- Sprint 1 traceability retained at 6 PBIs / 19 SP.
- Collaboration and academic evidence integrated into the report baseline.

## Evidence boundaries

This release does not mean:

- a final Mobile product;
- complete product validation;
- production deployment; or
- completion of later course milestones.
