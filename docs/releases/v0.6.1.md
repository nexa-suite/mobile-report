# Nexa Mobile Report v0.6.1

**AV1 Academic Hotfix**

## Scope

`v0.6.1` is a patch release over `v0.6.0`. It applies final AV1
rubric-alignment corrections without introducing new product scope or redefining
accepted Product, Lean UX, requirements or architecture semantics.

## Included

- Removed the residual Spike-specific Epic assignment from SPIKE-001.
- Strengthened Student Outcome 7.c1 evidence against the ABET criteria.
- Organized bibliography categories and updated SCM evidence metadata.
- Replaced the final Impact Mapping visual from the approved Chapter 02 input.
- Removed obsolete AV1 exposition-video placeholder wording.

## Preserved invariants

- Product Backlog: 97 PBIs / 426 SP.
- Backlog composition: 73 Mobile Functional, 6 Landing, 12 Technical and 6 Spikes.
- Four academic Sprints, including Sprint 1 at 6 PBIs / 19 SP.
- Needfinding research: 9/9 interviews, three per segment.
- Eleven accepted Bounded Contexts.
- Existing Lean UX, requirements, DDD, C4 and Tactical DDD semantics.

## Evidence boundary

This release stabilizes documented academic evidence only. It does not claim a
completed product, production readiness, deployment, implementation of the
backlog, or validation of future hypotheses.
