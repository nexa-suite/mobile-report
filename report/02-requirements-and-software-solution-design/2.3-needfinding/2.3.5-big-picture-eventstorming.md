# 2.3.5 Big Picture EventStorming

El Big Picture EventStorming se utilizará para observar el dominio de Nexa de extremo a extremo, sin dividirlo primero por aplicaciones. El flujo compartido parte de la habilitación del tenant y la relación con compradores, continúa con el catálogo y el compromiso comercial, y llega a la disponibilidad, el fulfillment, la entrega, los documentos, los pagos y la trazabilidad.

En esta vista Mobile representa diferentes puntos de interacción con el mismo dominio. No se crearán eventos de dominio para cámara, mapas, notificaciones push, almacenamiento local o sincronización; esos elementos pertenecen a la aplicación o a la integración, salvo que una sesión de dominio posterior demuestre una regla de negocio distinta.

## Event hypotheses for the target map

The following sequence is a working hypothesis for the workshop and is marked
`TARGET / NOT YET VALIDATED`. It is useful for checking the boundary between
commands, domain events, policies and read projections before the team conducts
the session.

| Order | Candidate domain event | Owning context | Possible command or trigger | Candidate consumer |
| ---: | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | Workforce Membership authorized | BC-01 | Resolve authorized work context | All tenant-scoped contexts |
| 2 | Buyer Relationship approved | BC-02 | Approve buyer relationship | BC-03, BC-04, BC-06 |
| 3 | Product/SKU policy published | BC-03 | Publish permitted catalog/policy snapshot | BC-04, BC-05 |
| 4 | Commercial Commitment confirmed | BC-04 | Confirm commitment | BC-05, BC-06, BC-07 |
| 5 | Inventory availability reserved or allocated | BC-05 | Reserve/protect or physically allocate inventory | BC-06, BC-11 |
| 6 | Dispatch Handoff recorded | BC-06 | Mark prepared work ready and hand off | Driver workflow, BC-10, BC-11 |
| 7 | Delivery outcome recorded | BC-06 | Record attempt/outcome | BC-09, BC-10, BC-11 |
| 8 | Buyer Receipt confirmed | BC-06 | Confirm quantities actually received | BC-09, BC-10, BC-11 |
| 9 | Discrepancy reported | BC-06 | Report difference without erasing facts | Source context, BC-07/08 only when a later policy applies |
| 10 | Notification delivery attempted | BC-10 | Deliver a permitted update | Buyer/operations projection |
| 11 | Business fact appended to timeline | BC-11 | Consume a confirmed source event | Authorized traceability readers |

These are not fabricated workshop results. The final board must record the
participants, date, source evidence, rejected alternatives and changes made by
the team.

El EventStorming anterior se conservará solo como referencia de trabajo. La versión actual debe alinearse con los 11 Bounded Contexts aceptados en `blueprint` y mantenerse separada de los diagramas estratégicos que se documentarán en la sección 2.5.
