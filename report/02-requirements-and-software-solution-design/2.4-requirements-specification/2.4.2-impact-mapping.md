# 2.4.2 Impact Mapping

## Purpose and status

The Impact Map connects an outcome to the actors who must change their
behavior, the product capabilities that may enable that change and the Mobile
V1 stories that express the candidate scope. The map below is a traceability
model, not evidence that the goals or impacts have already been validated.

The numeric targets, baselines and measurement window remain open until the
team completes the research campaign and agrees on a measurable business
baseline. No percentage or acceptance result is invented here.

## Traceability rules

1. A goal is not SMART merely because a target number is typed into this
   table; baseline, data source, window and owner must be agreed.
2. An impact is not a user quote. It must be supported by an interview or
   other identified evidence after manual review.
3. A deliverable may be a target design, an API contract or an implementation
   artifact, but its state must remain visible.
4. A Mobile story links to a goal without moving domain authority from its
   owning Bounded Context.
5. `MOB-US-044` belongs to Notifications for the notification capability;
   delivery, receipt, discrepancy and traceability facts remain distinct.

## Evidence register

- [ ] Goal owners assigned and recorded.
- [ ] One baseline and measurement source agreed for each accepted goal.
- [ ] Three to five current interviews completed per target segment or an
  explicit academic exception recorded.
- [ ] Impact Map captured in the accepted tool with date, participants and
  public/review link.
- [ ] Each behavior change reviewed against the corresponding stories and
  acceptance criteria.
