# 2.4.2 Impact Mapping

## Purpose and evidence boundary

This Impact Map connects a measurable outcome hypothesis with the actors whose
behavior may change, the candidate deliverables and the 28-story academic
Mobile V1 projection. It is a requirements and traceability artifact, not
evidence that an impact, goal or Product Acceptance has already been validated.

The map is source-backed by the current Mobile requirement projection and
catalog:

- blueprint/90-academic/mobile/course-1acc0238/requirements-projection.md
  defines the 28-row academic V1 projection, story points and S1/S2/S3 mapping.
- blueprint/03-mobile/requirements/mobile-v1-catalog.md defines story behavior
  and acceptance scenarios.
- blueprint/03-mobile/requirements/master-mobile-backlog.md preserves the
  73-story Product lifecycle projection.
- blueprint/01-shared/product/current-decisions.md preserves Product,
  authority, tenant and Mobile boundaries.

No interview result, metric, percentage, Board link or Product Acceptance is
inferred from those sources.

## Traceability rules

1. A goal becomes SMART only after baseline, signal, target, time window and
   owner are agreed and recorded; a placeholder-free sentence is not evidence
   of approval.
2. An impact hypothesis is not a user quote. Interview or other identified
   evidence must support it before validation is claimed.
3. A deliverable may be a target design, API compatibility projection or
   implementation artifact, but its evidence state remains visible.
4. A Mobile story links to an outcome without moving authority from its owning
   Bounded Context.
5. MOB-US-044 covers the Notifications capability; Delivery, receipt,
   discrepancy and Business Traceability facts remain distinct.
6. Metrics, baselines and targets must be derived from collected evidence; this
   document does not invent them.
