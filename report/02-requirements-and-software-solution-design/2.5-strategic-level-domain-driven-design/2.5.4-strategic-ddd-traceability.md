## Accepted model and provenance

The strategic model in this report follows the accepted Blueprint baseline. It
is not a claim that every context has already been implemented by the Mobile
team.

| Decision | Current interpretation | Report consequence |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Eleven Bounded Contexts accepted | Tenant & Access Governance; Customer & Buyer Relationships; Catalog & Commercial Policy; Sales Commitment; Inventory Availability; Fulfillment & Delivery; Credit & Receivables; Payments; Business Documents; Notifications; Business Traceability | All requirements and diagrams use these names and codes |
| Former ten-context proposal superseded | Notifications and Business Traceability have different ownership and failure semantics | They remain separate; no merged context is introduced |
| Mobile is a projection | Operations Mobile and Buyer Mobile are product surfaces/containers in the target view | No Mobile, Offline, Scanner, QR, Maps, Tracking, Push or IoT Bounded Context is created |
| PostgreSQL is physically shared | Ownership is logical by Bounded Context and access remains tenant-scoped | A report diagram must not show one database per context or Tenant |
| API is the authority | Commands, authorization, transactions and confirmed business facts remain server-side | Local state is limited to safe cache, harmless drafts, temporary evidence and retry metadata |
