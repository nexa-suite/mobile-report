##### Sprint Goal

> Our focus is on enabling the first coherent public and protected Mobile entry
> points for Nexa.
>
> We believe it delivers a clear company onboarding path, reliable authorized
> work context for Operations, safe product identification, and understandable
> delivery attention and handoff verification for B2B Buyers.
>
> This will be confirmed when a prospective company can understand Nexa and
> initiate onboarding, an authorized Operations user can return to the correct
> business context and identify a product using capture or manual fallback, and
> an authorized Buyer can identify a delivery requiring attention and verify its
> handoff without altering Driver facts.
