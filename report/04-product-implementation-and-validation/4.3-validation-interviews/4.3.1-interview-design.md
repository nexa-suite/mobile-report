# 4.3.1 Interview Design

## Validation protocol

| Segment | Participant target | Artifact to exercise | Minimum focus |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| S1 Field & Warehouse Operations | Warehouse Operator and Dispatch Coordinator; Sales Representative is deferred V2+ | Operations Mobile wireframe/prototype | Identification, lot/expiry, picking, readiness and Dispatch Handoff |
| S2 Delivery Workforce | Driver or Delivery Operator | Operations Mobile delivery flow | Assignment, navigation handoff, attempt, partial/rejected outcome, POD and handoff code |
| S3 B2B Buyers | Customer Buyer | Buyer Mobile handoff/receipt flow | Code verification, quantities, receipt, discrepancy and critical update |

Each session must record consent, participant identity, role, date, duration,
device/viewport, artifact version, tasks, observation versus interpretation,
quotes only with permission, issues and decision. Three to five current
participants per segment are required by the rubric; no participant or finding
is filled in by assumption.

The mixed-method plan is supported as a research method by [M2]: inquiry,
task/scenario testing and inspection answer different questions. The paper's
results are not transferred to Nexa and do not replace the team's own sessions.

## Session tasks

1. Resume an authorized work context.
2. Complete the main happy path for the assigned segment.
3. Recover from an invalid, unavailable or duplicate state.
4. Explain what the user believes was confirmed and what remains pending.
5. Rate clarity and confidence only after the preceding observations are
   recorded.
