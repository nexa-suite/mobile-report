# Glossary

The terms below use the current Nexa ubiquitous language. They are subject to
manual team review before final submission.

| Term | Definition in this report |
| :--- | :--- |
| Tenant | Maximum data and security boundary for a Nexa organization. |
| Workspace | V1 operational environment associated one-to-one with a Tenant; not a C4 container or a replacement for authorization. |
| Human Identity | Person identity used for authentication and attribution. |
| Workforce Membership | Authorization relationship between a Human Identity, Workspace and capabilities. |
| Customer Account | Commercial organization represented in Nexa. |
| Buyer Relationship | Authorized relationship between a Customer Account/person and a Tenant's commercial operation. |
| Product / SKU | Catalog identity used to distinguish a sellable or operational item. |
| Commercial Commitment | Business promise/order state; not a physical inventory allocation. |
| Inventory Reservation | Protection of available inventory for an authorized commitment. |
| Physical Allocation | Assignment of physical stock, lot or quantity to operational work. |
| Safety Stock | Inventory policy threshold used to protect availability; not an available quantity by itself. |
| Dispatch Handoff | Explicit transfer of prepared work from dispatch to delivery execution. |
| Delivery Outcome | Driver/operator record of what physically happened at a destination. |
| Buyer Receipt | Buyer record of quantities actually received. |
| Proof of Delivery | Evidence associated with an authorized delivery outcome. |
| Discrepancy | Difference reported between relevant delivery or receipt facts without erasing them. |
| Payment Reported | A payment assertion received from a person or external process. |
| Payment Confirmed | Payment state confirmed by the authoritative payment workflow. |
| Notification | Delivery intent/status for informing an authorized recipient. |
| Business Traceability | Durable representation of confirmed business facts across contexts. |
| Security Audit | Evidence of security-relevant access or action; distinct from business traceability. |
| AS-IS | Observed current state supported by a source, command or capture. |
| TARGET | Proposed future state or design projection. |
| OPEN / PENDING | Missing decision or evidence; not a completion status. |
| C4 | Architecture visualization model for system context, containers, components and deployment nodes. |
| CRUD | Create, read, update and delete; not a substitute for domain invariants or use-case boundaries. |
