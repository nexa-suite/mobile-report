## Annex G: Bibliographic Categories

This annex groups the sources cited in the report by their contribution to the
academic discussion. The complete APA 7 references appear in the Bibliography.

| Category | Representative sources | Contribution to the report |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Business and cold-chain context | INEI (2025a, 2025b), World Bank (2023), United Nations Environment Programme and Food and Agriculture Organization of the United Nations (2022), Mustafa et al. (2024), Zhou et al. (2025). | Frames the B2B, logistics, traceability, and cold-chain problem without defining an individual Nexa customer. |
| Competitive landscape | Riqra, Negocia.pe, Defontana, and Drivin. | Supports the comparison of B2B commerce, ERP, and transport-management offerings. |
| Research and UX methods | Ilhan (2025) and Maqbool and Herold (2024). | Supports the discussion of prototyping and usability-research methods without replacing study-specific evidence. |
| Traceability and technical reference | GS1 (2017), Arriaga-Lorenzo et al. (2023), Bravo De la Cruz et al. (2025), and De Lombaert et al. (2024). | Provides vocabulary and external context for traceability, physical operations, and product-condition questions. |

These categories organize sources for the reader. They do not transform a
secondary source into a user interview, a product decision, or evidence that a
mobile capability has been implemented.
